2

Where

Where You May Use It

You may display the badge on:

  • Your stoa’s website, including its footer, About page, or membership information.
  • Meeting announcements, flyers, newsletters, and social media graphics.
  • Presentations, handouts, and other materials identifying or promoting your stoa.

Keep your stoa’s own name or logo as the primary identity, with the Fellowship badge as a supporting membership mark.

Online, link the badge to www.stoicfellowship.com and use the alternative text “Member of The Stoic Fellowship.”

3

Website

Add It to Your Website

  1. Download your preferred SVG badge.
  2. Upload it to your website’s image or media folder.
  3. Add the image with your website builder, and link it to https://www.stoicfellowship.com.

If your website allows custom HTML, use the code below. Replace BADGE_SVG_URL with the address of the badge you uploaded. 

<a href="https://www.stoicfellowship.com">
  <img
    src="BADGE_SVG_URL"
    alt="Member of The Stoic Fellowship"
    width="300"
    style="display:block; max-width:100%; height:auto;"
  >
</a>

You may adjust the width to suit your layout. Keep the complete badge readable and preserve its proportions.

4

Consistent

Keep the Badge Consistent

Use the supplied artwork and preserve its proportions, wording, colors, and arrangement.

  • Resize the complete badge proportionally.
  • Leave clear space around it, about the height of the “MEMBER OF THE” lettering on every side.
  • Keep the lettering and emblem clearly readable.
  • Preserve the badge’s background and border.
  • Avoid stretching, cropping, recoloring, adding effects, or placing text over the badge.

If your printing method needs a single-color or simplified version, contact us for suitable artwork.

5

Logo & merch

The Standalone Logo and Merchandise

The standalone logo

The membership badge is the approved mark for identifying your stoa’s membership. Please request separate permission before using The Stoic Fellowship’s standalone logo, and continue to use your own stoa’s name and identity prominently.

The badge communicates membership. It does not indicate that The Stoic Fellowship operates your stoa or endorses every activity, publication, product, or opinion associated with it.

Merchandise and products

The standard badge may appear on your stoa’s flyers, meeting handouts, and other informational materials.

For clothing, mugs, bags, or other merchandise, whether sold or given away, please contact us before production. We can provide or approve artwork that pairs your stoa’s name with the membership badge and suits the production method. Keep your stoa’s identity prominent so people can recognize who produced the item.

6

Who

Who May Display the Badge?

This badge is for stoas whose membership has been confirmed by The Stoic Fellowship. Downloading the files does not establish membership or authorize their use.

Permission applies to your member stoa and cannot be transferred to another organization. If your membership ends or permission is withdrawn, remove the badge from your website and digital materials, and stop using it on newly produced items.
