We help people find them, start them, and run them well.

New to Stoicism?

Our mission is to connect you with other Stoics, ideally through a stoa in your own community. If Stoicism is new to you, it helps to get your bearings first — that's what Stoicism 101 is for. In just a few minutes, you'll learn the core ideas so you can walk into a group ready to join the conversation.
