# Study Stoicism — Resource Tagging Export

This is a full export of how every book and article in the Study Stoicism section of
thestoicfellowship.com/resources.html has been tagged for a new pillbox filter/search
feature, for a second opinion / cross-check.

## How the tags work

Each item gets a **Topic** tag (or tags) and a **Format** tag:

- **Topic** — one or more of: `ancient` (Ancient Philosophers primary texts), `modern`
  (Modern Authors' books), `logic` (Stoic Logic reading list), `physics` (Stoic Physics
  reading list), `beginner-ethics` (introductory/accessible ethics content),
  `advanced-ethics` (academic/scholarly ethics content). A book by a Modern Author
  carries BOTH `modern` and a level tag (`beginner-ethics` or `advanced-ethics`) — the
  level tag is what lets an individual book within an author's card show or hide
  independently based on the active filter, while the author's card itself stays
  visible as long as at least one of their books still matches.
- **Format** — `book` or `article`.

The two `beginner-ethics` / `advanced-ethics` tags are the least certain calls in this
whole pass — for most items there's no existing site content to base the level on, just
a title, an author, and (for reading-list entries) a one-line description. **These are
the assessments most worth a second opinion.** The `ancient` / `modern` / `logic` /
`physics` topic tags and the `book` / `article` format tags are structural (which
section of the page the item is already in) and are not really in question.

For each item below: **Title**, **Author/By-line**, **Link**, **Assigned tags**, and
any **existing site description** (the blurb already on the page, where one exists —
useful context for judging the level call). A **Rationale** column gives the reasoning
used for this pass, mainly for the level calls.
